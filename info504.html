1. About This Course

In this section of the website, we teach the 504 Essential Words book using a storytelling method. Instant translations help you understand each word more easily, while audio recordings improve your pronunciation. At the end of each lesson, you can take a quiz to test your knowledge and track your progress.

2. What You Will Learn

Each lesson contains about 12 new vocabulary words. You will learn each word's meaning, pronunciation, spelling, and how to use it correctly in sentences.

3. Learning Process

Read the story, review the translation, listen to the pronunciation, and complete the quiz. If you don't pass, simply review the lesson and try again until you feel confident.

4. Learning Tools

We provide stories, translations, pronunciation audio, vocabulary explanations, and quizzes to make learning easier and more enjoyable.

5. How Long Will It Take?

Everyone learns at a different pace, but with regular daily practice, you can finish the course faster than you might expect.
