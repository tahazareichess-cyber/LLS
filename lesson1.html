story of a simple man

Taha is a bachelor man who has keen mind to do sports and experiences new things.
Specially He likes to cycling with unaccustomed speed.
By the use of tact He can't abandon his gym
and He looks gallant man by the way He doesn't leave correct way even when everything forwards him to incorrect path.
Taha makes an effort to qualify for his football dreams but he has too many hardships
maybe some times he felt vacant space in his heart
but he has learned to take an oath that show himself how He decides to fight with jealous life.
Yes it's his history and short data of him.
